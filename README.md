# Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line

A one-sentence entry for the make.fun competition "Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line": a single sentence, at most 200 characters, that makes a small everyday moment strange and new.

The program in `main.py` writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt`, where the competition's sealed scorer reads it.

Competition: https://make.fun/api/competitions/u-pangg7-everyday-wonder-two-days-to-find-the-line-970432d3/requirements

## Entry

The lift doors opened on a stranger humming my favourite song, and for one floor the building felt like home.
